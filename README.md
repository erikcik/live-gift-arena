# live-gift-arena

Gift-reactive scenes for TikTok LIVE. Viewers comment, like and send gifts, and an animated scene on the stream
reacts in real time.

**Scene 1: Halay.** Type `halay` and you join the line. Gifts give you a mendil, a regional costume, the davul, or the
lead at the head of the line. A big gift makes the whole line squeeze together ("SIKIŞTIR!") with fireworks.

Status: **planning**. The research is in [`docs/research.md`](docs/research.md), and the build brief is in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
